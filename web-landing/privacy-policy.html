Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

1. Introduction

StudyTask Tracker ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the StudyTask Tracker mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information Collection and Use

We collect different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our App to you.

2.1 Types of Data Collected

Personal Identification Information:

  • Email Address: Collected during authentication via Google Sign-In
  • User ID (UID): Unique identifier assigned by Firebase
  • Profile Information: Your name and profile picture (via Google account)

Educational Data:

  • Task Information: Task titles, subjects, due dates, descriptions, and status
  • Submitted Files: Metadata about submitted files (name, upload timestamp, file references)
  • Google Classroom Integration: If you connect with Google Classroom, we store:
    • Classroom course information
    • Assignment data synchronized from Classroom
    • Your Classroom profile (name, email)

Technical Data:

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, features used, and timestamps
  • Location Data: Your approximate location (not currently stored, but may be collected with user permission in future versions)

2.2 Purpose of Data Collection

We collect and process data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve the App
  • To authenticate users and manage user accounts
  • To send push notifications and reminders about tasks
  • To sync and display your tasks from Google Classroom
  • To allow you to upload and manage submitted files
  • To perform analytics and understand user behavior
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

Your personal and educational data is stored in the following services:

  • Firebase Firestore: Cloud database for storing tasks, user settings, and file metadata
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure authentication service managed by Google
  • Google Cloud Storage: For storing submitted files (if applicable)

3.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmitted over the internet (SSL/TLS)
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Role-based access controls to Firebase services
  • Regular security audits and monitoring

4. Children's Privacy

Our App may be used by children under 13 years old. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and comply with applicable children's privacy laws including COPPA and similar regulations.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent
  • We use only necessary data for educational purposes
  • We provide tools for parents/guardians to manage their child's account
  • We do not use behavioral advertising targeting children
  • We do not sell personal data of children

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information about you:

5.1 Google Services

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase: For backend services including authentication, database, and analytics
  • Google Classroom API: For syncing educational tasks

5.2 Notification Service

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

  • With Your Consent: We may share your data for purposes you explicitly authorize
