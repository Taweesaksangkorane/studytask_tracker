StudyTask Tracker - Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 17, 2026

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to 6731503012@lamduan.mfu.ac.th with subject: Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the Google email address used to sign in to StudyTask Tracker.
  3. We will verify your request and process it within 7 business days.

Data that will be deleted

  • User account mapping and profile data in the app backend
  • Task data created or synced for your account
  • Uploaded assignment file metadata linked to your account

Data that may be retained

  • Minimal technical logs required for security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance
  • Retained for up to 90 days unless a longer period is required by law

Important note

Deleting your StudyTask Tracker account may not automatically delete data stored in your own Google account (for example, Google Classroom records). Manage that data through your Google account settings.
